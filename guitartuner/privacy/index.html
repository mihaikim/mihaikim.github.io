In short

Tuner doesn’t collect any personal data. There’s no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you is sent to me.

The microphone

Tuner uses the microphone only while the app is open, to hear the note you play. The sound is analysed on your iPhone as it comes in. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn the microphone off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

No internet needed

Tuner works offline. It doesn’t connect to any server.

Links

The Buy me a coffee link opens buymeacoffee.com in your browser. That site has its own privacy policy.
