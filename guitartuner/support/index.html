The tuner doesn’t hear my guitar

Tuner needs the microphone. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn on Tuner.

Then find a quiet spot, hold your phone close to the guitar, and pluck one string firmly and let it ring.

It shows the wrong note

A string that is far out of tune can sound like the next note along. Tap the string you’re tuning to lock it, or use Beginner mode, which listens for one string at a time.

How does Beginner mode work?

Tap Beginner at the top left, or Tune step by step in the How to tune page. The app starts with the thickest string and tells you in plain words whether to tighten or loosen it. If it says Wrong way, turn the peg the other way. When a string stays in tune for a second, it gets a check and the next string comes up.

Which tunings are there?

Standard, Drop D, Half step down, Full step down, DADGAD, Open G and Open D. Pick one in the menu above the strings.
