The map doesn’t show where I am

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Toilet Finder and choose While Using the App. The location button only shows when you’re in Paris.

A toilet is closed, missing or in the wrong place

The toilets come from the City of Paris open data. When you open the app and you’re online, it checks for a newer list, at most once a day. If something still looks wrong, email me the address.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The map of Paris and the list of toilets are saved on your phone. Only walking directions need the internet.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no in-app purchases. If it helped you, you can buy me a coffee.
