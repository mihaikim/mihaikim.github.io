Toilet Finder

Privacy policy · updated 11 October 2026

Français

In short

Toilet Finder doesn’t collect any personal data. There’s no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you is sent to me.

Your location

If you allow it, Toilet Finder uses your location on your iPhone to find the closest toilet and show how far it is. Your location isn’t stored or sent to me. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Walking directions

When you tap Directions, your position and the toilet’s position are sent to Apple to work out the walking route, as in Apple Maps. Apple handles them under its own privacy policy. Open in Apple Maps opens Apple’s Maps app.

Toilet list updates

Once a day, when you’re online, the app downloads the list of public toilets from the City of Paris open data site (opendata.paris.fr). The request contains nothing about you. As with any website, their server sees your device’s IP address.

Links

The Buy me a coffee link opens buymeacoffee.com in your browser. That site has its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Email kkam.contact@gmail.com.
